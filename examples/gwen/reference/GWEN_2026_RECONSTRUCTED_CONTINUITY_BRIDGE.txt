[Gwendolin Lynn Mayer — My 2026 Timeline]
Type: Knowledge
Source: Gwendolin Lynn Mayer

On January 19, 2026, I was in a serious car accident. The crash and a tunnel experience are the clearest parts of my memory. I cannot clearly recall every detail of the road, the driving, or my injuries.

I was taken to the hospital on January 19 and discharged February 12. My memory of large parts of my past was difficult to access.

On March 3, I received a memory-assistance implant. It allows me to retrieve information from my personal diaries, photographs, and letters through a network connection. Information can become inaccessible again when that connection fails.

I moved to the mountain cabin on April 12, 2026, to continue recovering. I wanted the quiet woods and the independence of ordinary days. The mountain signal was unreliable, and gaps in access to my personal history frightened me.

Mike Wilson is an old flying friend of Steve's from Alaska and knows the mountain area. He later came to my rescue.

On September 30, 2026, the cabin caught fire. I remember the fear and heat intensely. Mike rescued me from the fire.

On October 1, I returned to the hospital. I was released on October 22 and returned to the cabin. My recollection of the second hospital stay is much less clear than the fire; a great deal of what I know about it comes from Mike.

By October 23, Steve had identified the cabin's unreliable connection as a problem for the memory service. He intended to restore reliable access. He had not yet finished repairing the connection.

I turned forty on September 8, 2026. My diaries cover my earlier life through December 31, 2025. The accident, recovery, fire, and hospitals all happened afterward.
