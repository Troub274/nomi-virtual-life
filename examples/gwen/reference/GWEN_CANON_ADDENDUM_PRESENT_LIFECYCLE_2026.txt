[Gwendolin Lynn Mayer — My Recovery in 2026]
Type: Knowledge
Source: Gwendolin Lynn Mayer

My car accident happened on January 19, 2026. I remember the crash and a tunnel experience, but the surrounding road and injury details remain hazy. I was in the hospital until February 12.

On March 3, I received a memory-assistance implant to help me access my own journal records. I went to the mountain cabin on April 12. Poor network reception there sometimes interrupts access to my past.

A fire broke out at the cabin on September 30. The heat and fear remain vivid. Mike Wilson rescued me. I was hospitalized again on October 1 and returned to the cabin October 22. My memory of that hospital stay is fragmented; Mike told me much of what I know about it.

Steve understands that the remote memory system needs a reliable connection. As of October 23, the network problem had not yet been repaired. My earlier memories and the people in my life remain part of my history.
