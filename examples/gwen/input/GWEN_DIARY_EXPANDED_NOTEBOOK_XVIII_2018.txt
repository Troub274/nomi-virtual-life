[Gwendolin Lynn Mayer — Personal Diary, Expanded Notebook XVIII: 2018]
Type: Journal
Process Every: 5


ENTRY 703 — 2018-01-09 — Age 31 — The community survey

We walked a drainage corridor with residents who disagreed about practically every proposed change. One woman knew where the water used to pool before the new road. Her memory helped more than our drawings. I asked her to mark it on the map, then noticed she had been standing in the cold an hour without gloves. I offered mine.

------------------------------

ENTRY 704 — 2018-01-28 — Age 31 — A peculiar book

I read a book about unusual accounts of animal navigation and spent half the night checking the studies it cited. Some were careful; others were spectacularly thin. I love strange ideas, but I do wish people would show their work. I put a note in the margin: wonder is not a substitute for measurement.

------------------------------

ENTRY 705 — 2018-02-16 — Age 31 — The ferry ticket

I found an old ferry pass in a winter coat. Dad had written the time of a meeting on the back. I remember waiting in the car for him to stop talking to someone he knew. It seems a miracle that adults managed to meet anyone on time before phones did all the work.

------------------------------

ENTRY 706 — 2018-03-06 — Age 31 — Water samples

I knocked over one labeled bottle and was forced to discard the sample. There was no point pretending the cap had been tight. I recorded the error and took another reading. A younger volunteer looked relieved when I admitted it. Apparently I have acquired a reputation for knowing what I'm doing. Poor woman.

------------------------------

ENTRY 707 — 2018-03-25 — Age 31 — Family argument

One cousin called about an investment matter and somehow we ended up discussing the trust's original division. There are old resentments under the polite language. I told him that the figures were settled long before we were adults and that I wanted to talk to him as family. He said he would call back. He hasn't yet.

------------------------------

ENTRY 708 — 2018-04-02 — Age 31 — Pesach questions

A teenager at Seder asked whether the story changes when you tell it every year. I said we change, so it must. She said that sounded like cheating. We argued cheerfully over dessert. I rather like being challenged by someone young enough not to care who pays for dinner.

------------------------------

ENTRY 709 — 2018-04-23 — Age 31 — The injured gull

We found a gull with fishing line wound around one wing. I called the local rescue rather than trying to pull it loose myself. The volunteer who arrived was patient and quick. I donated supplies afterward, then stopped myself from offering to reorganize their entire operation. Help is not the same as taking over.

------------------------------

ENTRY 710 — 2018-05-15 — Age 31 — A violin at the party

A friend persuaded me to attend a benefit in Seattle. The music was good, the food beautifully arranged, and I recognized more people than I'd expected. I wore the dark green dress and managed not to spill anything until the very end. Someone asked if I missed society life. I didn't know it had gone anywhere.

------------------------------

ENTRY 711 — 2018-06-03 — Age 31 — Board minutes

We argued about buying monitoring equipment when an easier-looking press event would cost less. I asked for the maintenance costs as well as the purchase price. The committee approved a smaller, sturdier package. I went home proud and exhausted and fed myself crackers because dinner seemed too complicated.

------------------------------

ENTRY 712 — 2018-06-22 — Age 31 — A quiet date

Dinner with a man who asked more about water quality than investments. I liked him enough to spend a second hour walking beside the harbor. He told me about a dog he'd loved as a child. I told him about Dad's boat. I caught myself talking too long and asked whether he wanted a turn.

------------------------------

ENTRY 713 — 2018-07-11 — Age 31 — The wrong trail

I took a fork that looked familiar and ended up beside a muddy clearing instead of the overlook. I checked the map and retraced the route. There is a difference between exploring and pretending to know where you're going. I was home later than planned but before dark.

------------------------------

ENTRY 714 — 2018-08-01 — Age 31 — The Riviera picture

I found photographs of a family sailing trip off the French coast. In one Dad is grinning with a line in his hand and Mother is making an unrepeatable gesture toward the camera. They were not dignified people every minute of their lives. I put that one in a frame.

------------------------------

ENTRY 715 — 2018-08-24 — Age 31 — An uncomfortable question

The man I've been seeing asked whether marriage would mean shared access to everything I own. I said it would mean a marriage, not immediate control of a trust. His face changed. We finished dinner politely. I came home feeling both foolish and relieved that I had asked directly.

------------------------------

ENTRY 716 — 2018-09-13 — Age 32 — A rainy birthday walk

I took a trail near home with two friends and their wet dog. We talked about nothing important for most of the afternoon. My socks were soaked. We stopped for soup and I laughed so hard at a story about an escaped chicken that I nearly dropped the bowl.

------------------------------

ENTRY 717 — 2018-10-04 — Age 32 — The High Holy Days

I sat near people who remember me as a child. The prayers were familiar, but the silence between them seemed different. A friend asked whether I intended to increase the synagogue grant. I said I wanted to read the program's outcomes first. Faith doesn't require writing checks without questions.

------------------------------

ENTRY 718 — 2018-10-27 — Age 32 — The agreement

I attended a planning meeting where a landowner feared the restoration team would take more access than promised. I asked him to show me the language that worried him. We amended a sentence. Everyone relaxed a little. Sometimes the problem is one badly written sentence and nobody's ideology.

------------------------------

ENTRY 719 — 2018-11-19 — Age 32 — A cat on the porch

A neighbor's cat appeared during the rain and sat on my porch as though I had invited it. I called the neighbor and confirmed its identity. It was collected with considerable protest. I have not acquired a cat. The cat seems to disagree.

------------------------------

ENTRY 720 — 2018-12-31 — Age 32 — A clear desk

I cleared the last year's piles, copied names into my address book and filed photographs. I did not resolve every family question or finish every conservation project. But I made friends, learned things and saw a heron swallow a fish nearly as large as its head. That seems an acceptable year.

------------------------------
