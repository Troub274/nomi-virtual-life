ENTRY 775 — 2022-01-06 — Age 35 — Forms in triplicate

I spent a morning reviewing grant contracts and wished, briefly, that I'd gone to law school. Then I remembered the hours Dad worked and changed my mind. I asked our counsel for a clean explanation and got one. Knowing when to ask someone else is a skill.

------------------------------

ENTRY 776 — 2022-01-25 — Age 35 — The winter beach

I found tiny tracks in wet sand, then stood there trying to decide whether they belonged to a bird or a small mammal. The next wave erased them. I was absurdly disappointed. I wrote a description from memory, which is apparently how I intend to spend my life.

------------------------------

ENTRY 777 — 2022-02-11 — Age 35 — Dinner in Seattle

I went to dinner with friends from families I've known since school. We were all dressed properly and nobody behaved properly after dessert. The argument about music was the best part. I defended Coltrane, then spilled water onto my napkin.

------------------------------

ENTRY 778 — 2022-03-02 — Age 35 — Cousin correspondence

One of my cousins sent photographs of a family gathering. I replied warmly and tried not to make every exchange a referendum on the trust. We were children once. We also knew, even as children, that money could sit at the table without a chair.

------------------------------

ENTRY 779 — 2022-03-19 — Age 35 — The owl

An owl appeared at dusk near the trail junction. I stood quietly and let it decide whether to stay. It watched me for a moment, then moved into the trees. I walked home thinking about how much attention goes into simply being alive outdoors.

------------------------------

ENTRY 780 — 2022-04-16 — Age 35 — Passover seating

I hosted part of the holiday gathering. The seating chart took longer than the menu because several relatives were not speaking. I placed them far enough apart that dinner could proceed. We read, ate, debated and briefly laughed together. Nobody solved the family history.

------------------------------

ENTRY 781 — 2022-05-08 — Age 35 — A useful gift

I gave the wildlife rescue a grant for enclosure repairs after visiting and reviewing their plan. A young staff member showed me the drainage problem and explained why the proposed materials would last. She knew the work. I made sure the board heard from her directly.

------------------------------

ENTRY 782 — 2022-05-27 — Age 35 — The piano tuner

The tuner came and found one sticky key. He told me the piano was in fine condition if I played it more regularly. I agreed and promptly practiced for an hour. The next day I played for ten minutes. Consistency is still not my strongest virtue.

------------------------------

ENTRY 783 — 2022-06-21 — Age 35 — What to keep

I sorted boxes from Mother's closet. I donated clothes that were in good condition and kept one coat because it still smelled faintly of her perfume. I am not ashamed of an irrational decision about a coat. The closet has room now, which feels both good and wrong.

------------------------------

ENTRY 784 — 2022-07-09 — Age 35 — A sail in bright weather

We took the boat out early and watched clouds build beyond the shore. I handled lines, checked the forecast and stayed well within the day's plans. A friend asked why I didn't want to push farther. I said returning safely is also part of sailing.

------------------------------

ENTRY 785 — 2022-07-30 — Age 35 — The steep meadow

In Switzerland I walked to a viewpoint and lost the trail briefly where cattle had churned the path. A farm dog barked without moving from the shade. I waited until its owner waved me through. The sound of bells followed me downhill.

------------------------------

ENTRY 786 — 2022-08-17 — Age 35 — A curious letter

A distant acquaintance wrote offering an elaborate investment opportunity. The letter was full of urgency and flattery. I declined in two sentences and filed it. My father's daughter, certainly, but Mother's too: she would have made a marvelous joke about the stationery.

------------------------------

ENTRY 787 — 2022-09-08 — Age 36 — Thirty-six

I celebrated my birthday with a hike and late dinner. The restaurant was loud and the food wonderful. I wore sensible shoes with a nice dress. Nobody commented, which is how I know I have chosen the right friends.

------------------------------

ENTRY 788 — 2022-09-28 — Age 36 — The shofar

The sound stayed in my head after services. I thought about my father learning the melodies as an adult and how proud he was whenever I read Hebrew better than he did. I miss being able to tease him about that.

------------------------------

ENTRY 789 — 2022-10-20 — Age 36 — Public comment

At a meeting, a developer claimed our stream measurements were invented. I read the method and the independent verification aloud. He interrupted. I waited until he finished and repeated the dates. We can disagree about policy. We cannot pretend the samples are imaginary.

------------------------------

ENTRY 790 — 2022-11-04 — Age 36 — A snapped buckle

The buckle on my pack broke three miles into a hike. I improvised a repair with cord and returned by the shorter route. I was annoyed but also rather pleased. Dad taught me knots; Mother taught me that complaining is permitted if you fix the problem first.

------------------------------

ENTRY 791 — 2022-11-22 — Age 36 — A family recipe

I made Mother's holiday dish from a handwritten card and discovered she'd omitted the quantity of oil. I telephoned an aunt who said Mother never measured it. The result was delicious and not reproducible. I wrote down what I actually did before memory improved it.

------------------------------

ENTRY 792 — 2022-12-31 — Age 36 — The good work

The habitat project has better data and fewer spectacular promises than it did a year ago. I like that. My friendships feel sturdier as well. I haven't fallen in love this year and I haven't suffered for the absence of it. There was much to do and much to enjoy.

------------------------------
