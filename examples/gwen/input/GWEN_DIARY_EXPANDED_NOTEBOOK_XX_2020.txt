ENTRY 739 — 2020-01-10 — Age 33 — Field season plans

The team laid out sampling dates and equipment needs. I made a spreadsheet for replacement parts and left room for things we hadn't anticipated. Someone said my contingency column was pessimistic. I said it was a love letter to functioning equipment.

------------------------------

ENTRY 740 — 2020-02-02 — Age 33 — Little evidence

A friend sent a dramatic article about a mysterious animal sighting. I read it twice and then found the photograph had been taken years earlier somewhere else. I still love the possibility of strange things in the woods. I don't love being lied to about them.

------------------------------

ENTRY 741 — 2020-02-24 — Age 33 — A quiet ferry

The crossing was windy and the water stippled with rain. I watched a gull follow the boat's wake and thought about how much of my childhood was spent on this route. I can name certain turns of light better than certain streets in Seattle.

------------------------------

ENTRY 742 — 2020-03-16 — Age 33 — Everything cancelled

Meetings, travel and field visits have all begun disappearing from the calendar because of the virus. I called friends and checked on older members of the congregation. The grocery shelves looked oddly bare. I have supplies, but not the certainty that usually goes with having made a plan.

------------------------------

ENTRY 743 — 2020-03-30 — Age 33 — An unfamiliar Seder plan

We arranged to celebrate Pesach by video rather than gathering at one table. I tested the connection, made a list of readings and accidentally muted myself during rehearsal. Mother would have found the whole thing absurd and then taken charge of it.

------------------------------

ENTRY 744 — 2020-04-09 — Age 33 — Pesach apart

We held Seder from separate homes. The sound was imperfect and somebody's child kept disappearing under the table. I missed passing plates from hand to hand. Still, we told the story and argued about the words, and the little faces on the screen made me unexpectedly happy.

------------------------------

ENTRY 745 — 2020-04-28 — Age 33 — Hands and distance

I dropped supplies at a neighbor's door and talked from farther away than felt polite. She said she was glad to see me even at a distance. I came home and washed my hands again, then looked out at the cedars. The trees have no idea we're all frightened.

------------------------------

ENTRY 746 — 2020-05-19 — Age 33 — Remote monitoring

We arranged a safe, limited schedule for essential site checks. The sensors kept reporting, but a sensor never tells the entire story. I wrote down which observations were missing rather than pretending the graphs were complete.

------------------------------

ENTRY 747 — 2020-06-06 — Age 33 — An argument online

Someone accused our project of hiding information. I drafted a furious reply, saved it, took a walk and returned with a shorter answer linking to the public measurements. Being right is not an excuse to be cruel. Nor is being charitable a reason to let nonsense stand.

------------------------------

ENTRY 748 — 2020-06-25 — Age 33 — Birdsong morning

With fewer cars on the road I noticed different sounds through the open window. Perhaps there are more birds, perhaps I'm simply listening more carefully. I recorded three calls and checked them against a field guide. I got one wrong and enjoyed correcting it.

------------------------------

ENTRY 749 — 2020-07-14 — Age 33 — Another cancelled trip

There will be no usual summer in Switzerland. I looked at photographs instead, including a picture of Mother laughing at a cow that had wandered too near our picnic. I miss traveling. I also know missing travel is a very fortunate kind of hardship.

------------------------------

ENTRY 750 — 2020-08-03 — Age 33 — The budget call

A nonprofit asked for emergency support. I reviewed salaries, cash flow and services, then agreed to a targeted grant. I won't pretend the amount is a heroic sacrifice for me. The workers keeping the program alive are doing the difficult part.

------------------------------

ENTRY 751 — 2020-08-29 — Age 33 — The boat

I checked on the boat and found a rope stiff with salt and age. I replaced it and spent time wiping down surfaces that were already reasonably clean. Maintenance is soothing because the results are visible. Grief and public health emergencies are rather less cooperative.

------------------------------

ENTRY 752 — 2020-09-08 — Age 34 — Thirty-four

Birthday cake arrived on the porch, courtesy of friends determined to make an occasion out of a strange year. We talked outdoors with chairs spread apart. I wore the ridiculous otter scarf from years ago. There were no candles to blow across the cake, which everyone agreed was wise.

------------------------------

ENTRY 753 — 2020-09-27 — Age 34 — The holy days at home

I listened to prayers through speakers. A familiar melody brought back my father's low voice from a room on Bainbridge, though I could not have said which year. I wrote down the memory before it faded. I am grateful for rituals sturdy enough to survive changes in form.

------------------------------

ENTRY 754 — 2020-10-18 — Age 34 — A field mistake

I misread a timestamp on a remotely uploaded sample and wrote an incorrect note. I caught it in the review. I informed the team and corrected the dataset, including the audit trail. Nothing catastrophic happened. I still spent twenty minutes annoyed with myself.

------------------------------

ENTRY 755 — 2020-11-12 — Age 34 — Four years

I remembered Mother with her favorite tea and a phone call to Brooklyn. An aunt told me an old family story that contradicted Mother's version. I said I was keeping both. We laughed. I think she would have approved.

------------------------------

ENTRY 756 — 2020-12-31 — Age 34 — A smaller world

The year has been narrow in miles and enormous in consequences for other people. I am fortunate to have shelter, work and people to call. I have not learned a grand lesson. I have learned to ask after neighbors and check the date on samples twice.

------------------------------
