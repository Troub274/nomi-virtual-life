[Gwendolin Lynn Mayer — Personal Diary, Expanded Notebook XXI: 2021]
Type: Journal
Process Every: 5

AUTHORSHIP NOTE: Original fictional first-person diary. Later friendships, romantic relationships, and projects are provisional unless established by Steve. No 2026 memory-loss event or explanation is added.

ENTRY 757 — 2021-01-08 — Age 34 — Winter bird count

I joined a small bird count with distancing precautions. We debated whether a quick dark shape was a starling or something else and left it unidentified. I prefer an honest blank on the list to a confident mistake.

------------------------------

ENTRY 758 — 2021-01-28 — Age 34 — A familiar voice

One of Mother's old friends called simply to chat. She remembered Mother as a formidable negotiator over charity auction details, which delighted me. People tend to speak gently about the dead. I like stories that preserve their sharp edges.

------------------------------

ENTRY 759 — 2021-02-14 — Age 34 — No flowers required

I spent Valentine's Day helping a friend repair a broken shelving unit and then eating takeaway. There was no romantic disaster, either. I thought of old engagements without wanting to return to any of them. It was an ordinary pleasant Sunday.

------------------------------

ENTRY 760 — 2021-03-06 — Age 34 — The footbridge

A trail bridge had lost planks over the winter. We reported the condition and took the longer route home. Somebody suggested we could cross anyway. I said no one should have to risk an ankle for our photographs. The old me might have tried it. The current me had dry socks and an appointment tomorrow.

------------------------------

ENTRY 761 — 2021-03-27 — Age 34 — Pesach logistics

I helped organize food deliveries for families unable to join larger gatherings. The logistics were fiddly and several addresses were wrong. A volunteer caught the final one before the driver left. I wrote her a thank-you note rather than merely adding a check to the budget.

------------------------------

ENTRY 762 — 2021-04-15 — Age 34 — A new sensor

The replacement monitor has an infuriating interface and excellent calibration. I spent three hours with the manual and finally got it configured. The water readings are more useful than the device's personality. I went outside to look at actual water afterward.

------------------------------

ENTRY 763 — 2021-05-04 — Age 34 — The first hug

I hugged a friend after we both felt comfortable doing so. I hadn't realized how much I missed the easy physical grammar of friendship. We stood awkwardly afterward, wondering whether we should have made it ceremonial. Then we laughed.

------------------------------

ENTRY 764 — 2021-05-23 — Age 34 — An invitation

A man I'd known through conservation work asked me to dinner. I said yes and suggested a place where we'd be able to hear each other. He said he knew it. I was delighted that neither of us had to explain why conversation mattered more than the view.

------------------------------

ENTRY 765 — 2021-06-12 — Age 34 — A landowner's dog

The dog accompanied our site walk and insisted on checking every measurement stake. Its owner was apologetic. I said I preferred this sort of supervision to a committee chair breathing down my neck. The dog was polite enough not to disagree.

------------------------------

ENTRY 766 — 2021-07-02 — Age 34 — Summer travel decisions

I considered going abroad and then decided on a smaller trip within Washington. I don't enjoy making plans merely to prove that I can. A few nights by the mountains will do nicely, assuming I remember to pack the raincoat this time.

------------------------------

ENTRY 767 — 2021-07-24 — Age 34 — A long dinner

The conservation colleague and I talked for four hours. He's thoughtful and has a contagious laugh. I caught myself relaxing in a way I hadn't for some time. Later he asked about my family; I told him about the piano and the boat before I told him about money.

------------------------------

ENTRY 768 — 2021-08-14 — Age 34 — Something difficult

I overheard an offhand remark that suggested he'd discussed my financial circumstances with someone else. I asked him directly. He apologized and said he hadn't meant harm. I believe he was careless, not malicious. I also told him it must not happen again.

------------------------------

ENTRY 769 — 2021-09-08 — Age 35 — Thirty-five

The birthday cake had a small bird made of icing, which looked more like a wounded potato. I loved it. Friends came over, and the evening turned into a debate about whether shore crabs recognize individual predators. It was the sort of party I enjoy.

------------------------------

ENTRY 770 — 2021-09-17 — Age 35 — Days of Awe

I spent time thinking about how easy it is to want to be forgiven and how difficult it is to listen when someone tells you what you did. I wrote letters to two people I had neglected. Neither letter asked for anything in return.

------------------------------

ENTRY 771 — 2021-10-11 — Age 35 — A boundary

The man I've been seeing suggested putting me in touch with his business partner. The proposal had little to do with conservation and a great deal to do with my assets. When I declined, he persisted. I ended the evening early. I was not uncertain about what I'd heard.

------------------------------

ENTRY 772 — 2021-10-28 — Age 35 — After the boundary

He called to explain. I listened, asked two questions and said I didn't want to continue seeing him. My voice was calm enough to surprise him. I cried in the kitchen afterward, mostly because I had liked him. I washed a cup that was already clean.

------------------------------

ENTRY 773 — 2021-11-16 — Age 35 — Back to a river

I walked by water moving over stones and felt better without needing to describe why. A kingfisher crossed the channel. I watched it until it disappeared. I have the ridiculous privilege of being able to take an afternoon like that when I need one.

------------------------------

ENTRY 774 — 2021-12-31 — Age 35 — A good many small things

We repaired monitoring stations, supported our congregation, saw friends again, and argued over maps. I have been disappointed in one person and delighted by many others. Those facts can share a year without one cancelling the other.

------------------------------
