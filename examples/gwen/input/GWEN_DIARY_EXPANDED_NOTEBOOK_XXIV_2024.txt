ENTRY 811 — 2024-01-04 — Age 37 — Morning routine

I have begun keeping a regular morning page: weather, appointments, something I hope to learn and one thing I actually noticed. It is not particularly noble. It stops me from losing scraps of experience under administrative papers.

------------------------------

ENTRY 812 — 2024-01-23 — Age 37 — Someone new

A friend introduced me to a man who liked animals and seemed genuinely interested in where I'd been hiking. We talked about ordinary things. I kept waiting for him to ask about money, which was unfair to both of us. I tried to remain present instead.

------------------------------

ENTRY 813 — 2024-02-13 — Age 37 — The second conversation

We met for coffee again. He was late because his dog had needed the veterinarian. I believed him; he showed me a photograph of the dog wearing a cone. He has a nervous laugh. I found it sweet.

------------------------------

ENTRY 814 — 2024-03-01 — Age 37 — Storm damage

A section of path washed out near our monitoring site. We made a temporary closure and documented the damage. Someone complained about the inconvenience. I understood the frustration and still supported keeping people off unstable ground.

------------------------------

ENTRY 815 — 2024-03-20 — Age 37 — In my mother's handwriting

I opened the baby book and reread her note about Dad crying when I was born. She wrote down ridiculous details, as though they mattered. They matter. I wish I could thank her for all the small observations she thought to save.

------------------------------

ENTRY 816 — 2024-04-23 — Age 37 — A long Seder

The child who once argued with me about repetition is now old enough to lead part of the reading. She still argues. I hope she always does. We were late eating and everyone blamed the discussion. I enjoyed it enormously.

------------------------------

ENTRY 817 — 2024-05-07 — Age 37 — A difficult disclosure

The man I've been seeing asked about family finances after hearing a remark at a dinner. I told him enough to be honest and made clear that I handle my own affairs. He said that was reasonable. For the first time in a while, I felt no need to apologize for the boundary.

------------------------------

ENTRY 818 — 2024-05-29 — Age 37 — Rocks in the stream

I spent two hours with a volunteer examining how water moved around a newly placed structure. We disagree about one pool's likely behavior at high flow. We wrote down both predictions so we can test them after rain. I like arguments that have a method for being settled.

------------------------------

ENTRY 819 — 2024-06-16 — Age 37 — An ordinary date

We made dinner together and burned the first batch of vegetables. He was more concerned about saving the pan than impressing me. I liked that. We ate sandwiches instead and watched the light fade from the garden.

------------------------------

ENTRY 820 — 2024-07-05 — Age 37 — A crowded ferry

A family lost a child's mitten and half the passengers joined the search. It turned up beneath a seat just before we docked. The child was triumphant. I found myself smiling for most of the drive home.

------------------------------

ENTRY 821 — 2024-07-24 — Age 37 — Mountain flowers

I photographed flowers on a summer hike and later discovered half the pictures were out of focus because the lens had a smear. I have one excellent image and many artistic blurs. I shall call the trip successful.

------------------------------

ENTRY 822 — 2024-08-18 — Age 37 — A strange request

My companion asked whether I could guarantee a loan for someone he knew. I said no and asked why he thought it appropriate to ask. He explained at length. I listened carefully, then repeated no. He seemed surprised that I did not become angry.

------------------------------

ENTRY 823 — 2024-08-25 — Age 37 — Afterward

He returned to the subject twice. I ended the relationship. I liked him and believed his affection was real in some respects, but I cannot let affection become access to my assets. I was very quiet when I told him. I cried afterward. That isn't a contradiction.

------------------------------

ENTRY 824 — 2024-09-08 — Age 38 — Thirty-eight

Friends brought a cake and forbade me to discuss anyone who had asked for a loan. We ate outdoors until the air cooled. A moth landed on the table beside my plate and I spent several minutes examining its markings instead of opening presents.

------------------------------

ENTRY 825 — 2024-10-12 — Age 38 — The old ferry road

I drove a familiar route from childhood and noticed two houses replaced by larger ones. The trees seemed fewer. I stopped to write down what I remembered, knowing memory is an imperfect survey instrument. Still, it deserves a line in the notebook.

------------------------------

ENTRY 826 — 2024-10-24 — Age 38 — The High Holy Days

The service was full and I stayed afterward to talk with a family new to the congregation. They asked about the children's program and I introduced them to someone who actually runs it. Writing checks is easy. Knowing who does the work takes attention.

------------------------------

ENTRY 827 — 2024-11-15 — Age 38 — The tiny rescue

I moved a soaked earthworm from pavement into damp soil and a passerby laughed kindly. I told him it was a very small act of public service. He replied that every city needs one. We both went on with our days.

------------------------------

ENTRY 828 — 2024-12-31 — Age 38 — A full year

I traveled, worked, loved someone briefly, lost some illusions and kept most of my hope. The shelves are too full of books and I have started using the dining table for maps again. I will tidy it tomorrow. Probably.
