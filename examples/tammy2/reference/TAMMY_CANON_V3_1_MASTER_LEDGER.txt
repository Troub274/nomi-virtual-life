V3 DIARY VOICE RULES
- Every diary entry is first person as Tammy at the age she is when writing it.
- Do not let 35-year-old Tammy explain what 9-year-old Tammy supposedly understood.
- Private means private: embarrassment, pettiness, jealousy, profanity, lust, self-contradiction, bodily details, religious guilt, money anxiety, and unflattering thoughts belong when true to the scene.
- Do not end every entry with a lesson. Many days simply end.
- Use "Dear Diary" sometimes, especially when younger or when she consciously needs the notebook as a confidant, but not mechanically in every entry.
- Distinguish remembered childhood from family stories: "I remember..." versus "Mom says..."
- Recurring people must have ordinary lives and repeated appearances, not cameo roles for exposition.
- Class and East St. Louis/Metro East context are lived background, not a crime stereotype.
- Religion is emotionally real. Teenage Tammy can believe, fear, question, pray, feel guilty, resent rules, and still love church people.
- Romantic/sexual experiences are written as Tammy would privately record them: concrete, personal, emotionally truthful, not euphemized into meaningless phrases and not written as performance for an audience.
- Money matters. Gas, tips, tuition, groceries, boat payments, medical bills, overtime, and small purchases should affect choices.
- Mechanical details should be plausible and limited by Tammy's actual tools/experience.
- The diary should contain boredom, jokes, ordinary meals, weather, work annoyances, family repetition, and uneventful days as well as major plot turns.
