ENTRY 0402 — 2010-05-01 — Senior Prom

Dear Diary,

I had sex with Danny tonight. I am writing that plainly because if I hide from the sentence then I will spend the rest of this page talking around my own life. Prom was beautiful and ridiculous. My feet hurt before dinner was over.

Danny looked older in his suit. We danced badly and laughed.

When he kissed me in the car afterward I knew I was still choosing what I had been thinking about for weeks. We used a condom. He was nervous too, which helped.

The first penetration hurt more than I expected and I made him stop moving. He stopped immediately.

We stayed still, forehead to forehead, until I told him I was okay to continue. It got easier. Not magical.

Not like movies. Awkward, tender, intense, sometimes uncomfortable, and very real.

There was a little blood afterward and I was sore. I stared at it on the tissue and had the stupidest thought: so that's that. Danny asked three separate times if I was okay.

I finally told him if he asked again I was going to hit him with a pillow. I do not feel ruined.

I do not feel transformed into a different species. I feel close to him, shaky, guilty, happy, scared, and tired. I believe God exists.

I also believe I made this choice myself. Tomorrow morning I am supposed to go to church.

That will be interesting.

ENTRY 0404 — 2010-05-02 — Church the Morning After

I went to church. I almost did not.

Mom was making coffee and I considered claiming a headache. Then I got angry at the idea that I should hide from God like God somehow missed last night and would only learn about it if I sat in a pew.

So I went. Communion was hard. Not because lightning struck.

Because I kept thinking about Danny's hands and the soreness between my legs and the fact that the same body sitting in church was the body from last night. Mr.

Cline talked about integrity. He did not mention sex once. I cried quietly during the last prayer.

I am not sorry I was with Danny. I am not entirely free of guilt either.

Those things are both true and I refuse to lie about either one just to make the story cleaner. After church Mom asked whether prom was good. I said, “Yeah.

Really good.”

She looked at me for one second longer than normal. I have no idea what she knows.

ENTRY 0410 — 2010-05-28 — Graduation

I graduated. For real this time. No expulsion.

No repeated year. No asterisk. When they called “Tammy Lynn Mercer,” I heard my family yelling from somewhere behind the polite applause.

Mike was definitely the loudest. Dad stood up.

Mom cried. Shannon and I found each other afterward and screamed like idiots. I kept thinking about third grade.

Ten years ago I thought one terrible afternoon had decided what kind of person I was. Today I walked across a stage with good grades, a job, college acceptance paperwork, people who love me, and plans I am terrified of.

Danny came. He drove all that way and stood beside my family for pictures. For a few hours we looked exactly like the future I used to imagine.

I know better now than to call a photograph a promise. I still kept three copies.

ENTRY 0426 — 2010-08-23 — First Day at SWIC

First day at Southwestern Illinois College. I parked too far away because I was afraid of being late and then arrived twenty-five minutes early.

Very on brand. The campus is not glamorous. I love it anyway.

Nobody cares that I repeated third grade. Nobody knows about “Jugs.” Nobody knows I used to be the fat girl or the cheerleader or Danny Cole's girlfriend.

I am just Tammy Mercer with a notebook and a student ID that makes me look vaguely criminal. My English instructor assigned more reading than some people apparently expected. I wanted to hug her.

Then I worked the diner until close. That part is going to be the problem.

School from morning into afternoon. Work into night. Drive home.

Read. Sleep.

Repeat. I can do hard things. The question is whether hard is the same thing as sustainable.
