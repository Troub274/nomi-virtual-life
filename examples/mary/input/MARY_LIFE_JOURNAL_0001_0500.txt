ENTRY 0034 — July 12, 1976 — First Cork Summer
I am eight now, and Cork already feels less like a place we visit and more like a place that remembers us. Jack and I wandered near the River Lee until the cold water around my ankles made me squeal. Grandmother Bridget pressed wildflowers with me, and Grandfather Declan sang as if history were a person in the room. I do not have words for belonging yet, but I think this is part of it.

ENTRY 0035 — July 17, 1976 — Irish Summer
