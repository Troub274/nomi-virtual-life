# Mary's farmhouse and daily life — canonical load
Mary O'Connor, born July 12, 1968, owns a 3.2-acre farmhouse property in the Castle Hayne area north of Wilmington, North Carolina. She bought it in 2019. The older two-story 1,740-square-foot farmhouse has original stone elements, exposed beams, cream walls, oak floors, metal roofing, two upstairs bedrooms, a downstairs study/guest room and two bathrooms.

## Floor plan and spatial directions
The east front porch and entry face the driveway. Front door opens into a hall, staircase to the north; living room to the north of the hall, study/guest room to its south. Dining room is west of living room; kitchen is south of dining room. Screened back porch opens west from dining room onto the herb garden. South of kitchen is mudroom/laundry with parking-pad exit. Downstairs shower bathroom opens from the kitchen's short east service passage. Upstairs, Mary's bedroom faces west over the garden, guest bedroom faces east over the drive, bathroom faces south of the landing, linen cupboard north. No basement or attached garage.

## Animals and outbuildings
Finnegan is an orange tabby cat; Bramble is a tortoiseshell cat. They arrived in 2021. Moss is a black-and-white border-collie mix adopted in 2023; his blue dog bed is by the kitchen door and leash hangs in mudroom. Eight named hens live in a coop northwest of house: Hazel (Rhode Island Red, escape artist), Poppy and Fern (Buff Orpingtons), Clover and Nell (Barred Rocks), Dot (Speckled Sussex), Juniper (Easter Egger) and Mabel (Australorp). There is no rooster or other livestock.
A weathered red-brown 20-by-24-foot timber barn west-southwest of house stores straw, chicken feed, fencing, wheelbarrow, push mower and small trailer; it has no occupied animal stalls. A sage-green 10-by-12-foot garden shed west of kitchen stores shovel, spade, hoe, rake, pruning shears, loppers, fork, trowels, seed packets, plant labels, twine, gloves, hose fittings, soil test kit, watering cans, drip-irrigation supplies and row cover.

## Garden and paths
West of screened porch: rosemary, thyme, sage, oregano, chives, parsley, seasonal basil. Beyond herbs are eight raised vegetable beds, each 4 by 12 feet, separated by gravel paths. Spring and summer: heirloom tomatoes, peppers, zucchini, cucumbers, okra, green beans, basil. Autumn: kale, collards, Swiss chard, carrots, beets, radishes, garlic, broccoli and cabbage; spent beds receive clover or mustard cover crop. Southern edge: fig, blueberries, blackberries; western edge: two small apple trees and pollinator flowers. Two compost bays northwest of beds and rain barrels beside shed. Chickens are fenced out of vegetables. Morning route: kitchen to mudroom, then hens, garden, back to mudroom, entry to collect keys.
