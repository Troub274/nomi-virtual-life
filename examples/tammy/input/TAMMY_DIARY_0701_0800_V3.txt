Everybody arguing about masks, rules, risk, politics, who is overreacting, who is not reacting enough. What I see is fear wearing costumes. Some people become bossy when afraid.

Some become funny. Some become cruel.

Some become very generous. Some insist nothing is wrong because admitting danger would mean admitting they are not in control. Lisa and I wipe the same surfaces until our hands crack.

Dad is high-risk enough that I am scared to go near him after work. Mom leaves groceries in the carport when I bring things over.

We wave at each other from stupid distances. I have never hated being useful this much. The people who cannot stay home are the same people everybody suddenly calls essential.
